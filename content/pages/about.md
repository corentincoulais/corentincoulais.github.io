Our research lab focuses on the understanding of the physics of soft structured active materials.

The goal is to develop the physics of **Machine Materials**, i.e. artificial materials which combine microstructure and out-of-equilibrium processes to interact with their environment in a programmable fashion. We are located at the [Institute of Physics](https://iop.uva.nl) of the University of Amsterdam and belong to the [vibrant Soft Matter Group](https://iop.fnwi.uva.nl/scm/).
