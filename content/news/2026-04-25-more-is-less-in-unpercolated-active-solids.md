We find out that when the building blocks of an active material become *more* active, the structure as a whole may actually become *less* active. Published in [PRX](https://journals.aps.org/prx/abstract/10.1103/flhb-kjyd) and featured in a [Physics Viewpoint by Tzer Han Tan](https://physics.aps.org/articles/v19/49)!
