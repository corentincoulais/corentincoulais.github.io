Usually, materials are either stiff or can absorb vibrations well – but rarely both. Here, we exploit buckling to make metamaterials that are *both* stiff and good at absorbing vibrations. [Out in Advanced Materials](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/adma.202301747). See also [this press release](https://iop.uva.nl/content/news/2023/06/buckle-up-a-new-class-of-materials-is-here.html).
