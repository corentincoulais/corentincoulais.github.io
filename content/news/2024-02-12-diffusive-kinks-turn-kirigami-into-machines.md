We introduce viscoelastic kirigami whose response depend on strain rate. We observe the emerge of diffusive kinks. We use those kinks to achieve basic mechanical tasks such as shape morphing, sensing and moving objects. Out in [Nature Communications](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-024-45602-7)!
