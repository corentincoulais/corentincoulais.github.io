We discover *wave coarsening*: in active solids, vibrations emerge and spontaneously grow in wavelength, period and amplitude, until the whole material oscillates in sync as a time crystal that moves to its own internal beat rather than an external one. We observe it in a one-dimensional ring and in a two-dimensional surface made of mechatronic building blocks with non-reciprocal interactions. Out in [Nature](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-11163-6)! Read more in the [IoP news story](https://iop.uva.nl/news/2026/time-crystals-moving-to-an-internal-beat).
