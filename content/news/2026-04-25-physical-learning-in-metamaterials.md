We devise active metamaterials that don’t just learn to change shape, but can autonomously adapt their shape-changing strategy, perform reflex actions and move around like living systems do. Published in [Nature Physics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03226-2) and featured in a [News and Views article by Karen Alim](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41567-026-03256-w)!
